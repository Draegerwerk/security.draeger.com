### [Infinity Acute Care System and Infinity M540 security vulnerabilities](<advisories/PSA-19-255-02_Product-Security-Advisory-IACS-VG4.1.pdf>)

PSA-19-255-2 · 2019-09-12

An attacker in the network can spoof or change data of the Infinity Acute Care System and the standalone Infinity M540, or send too much data to the system. The Cockpit can reboot and alarm settings can change, but the M540 keeps its essential performance. Software version VG4.1.1/VG4.0.3 and lower are affected.
