### [FragAttacks Wi-Fi vulnerabilities in Infinity M300, M540, Delta series and Vista 120 S](<advisories/2021-09-03-PSA-21-246-1_Security_Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-21-246-1 · 2021-09-03

FragAttacks is a group of design faults and implementation faults in the Wi-Fi 802.11 standard. Dräger tested two M540 versions, two M300 versions and one Delta version. Each tested product has at least one of these vulnerabilities. The advisory lists Infinity M300, Infinity M540, Infinity Delta series and Vista 120 S as affected.
