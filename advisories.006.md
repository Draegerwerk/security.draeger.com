### [Infinity Delta family security vulnerabilities in Delta, Delta XL, Kappa and Infinity Explorer C700](<advisories/2019-01-22-draeger-infinity-delta-vf10-1-security-advisory.pdf>)

PSA-19-001-1 · 2019-01-22

Several vulnerabilities exist in the Dräger Infinity Delta, Delta XL and Kappa patient monitors and in the Infinity Explorer C700, all software versions. A malformed network packet can make a monitor reboot. Log files are readable over an unauthenticated network connection. On the C700 an attacker can leave the kiosk mode, and outdated third-party components give more attack paths.
