### [Infinity Acute Care System and Infinity M540 security vulnerabilities](<advisories/PSA-19-255-03_Product-Security-Advisory-IACS.pdf>)

PSA-19-255-3 · 2022-01-25

An attacker can spoof or change the data that the Infinity M540 monitor and the IACS Cockpit send and receive. Too much network data can also make the Cockpit reboot. The advisory lists Infinity Acute Care System VG4.1.1/VG4.0.3 and lower and standalone M540 VG4.1.1 and lower; version VG4.2 is partially affected. The M540 keeps its essential performance.
