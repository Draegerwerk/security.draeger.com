### [PHP Security Vulnerabilities in Dräger Safety Products](<advisories/PSA-26-296-1-PHP-Security-Vulnerabilities.pdf>)

PSA-26-296-1 · 2026-10-23

The PHP project published two critical vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. They can give remote code execution without authentication. Dräger still investigates which Dräger Safety products are affected. Until now the only possible product is the Dräger REGARD 7000 OPC UA Server. Advisory PSA-26-295-1 gives more data.
