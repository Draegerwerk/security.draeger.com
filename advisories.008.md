### [Infinity Delta family security vulnerabilities, update 1 of PSA-19-001-1: fixed software versions named](<advisories/2019-03-07-draeger-infinity-delta-vf10-1-security-advisory-update-1.pdf>)

PSA-19-001-1-1 · 2019-03-07

This update 1 repeats the vulnerabilities of the Infinity Delta, Delta XL and Kappa patient monitors and the Infinity Explorer C700, and now names the fixed versions. A malformed network packet can reboot a monitor. Service log files are readable without authentication. On the C700 an attacker can leave the kiosk mode and use outdated components. Users must update to Delta VF10.1 and C700 VF9.1.1.
