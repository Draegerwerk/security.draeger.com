### [NUCLEUS:13 advisory: Dräger medical devices are not affected](<advisories/PSA-21-315-1_Nucleus13-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-21-315-1 · 2021-11-12

Forescout Research reported 13 vulnerabilities in Nucleus NET, the TCP/IP stack of the Siemens Nucleus real-time operating system. No Dräger medical device or its infrastructure uses the Nucleus TCP/IP stack or the Nucleus operating system. Therefore Dräger medical devices are not affected by the NUCLEUS:13 vulnerabilities.
