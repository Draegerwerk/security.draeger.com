### [Open source network stack vulnerabilities NAME:WRECK - Dräger medical devices are not affected](<advisories/2021-04-19-PSA-20-109-01-NAME-WRECK.pdf>)

PSA-21-109-1 · 2021-04-19

Forescout made public nine vulnerabilities in the DNS functions of four open source network stacks. Dräger medical devices and the related infrastructure do not use the affected stack versions on their external communication interfaces. Therefore Dräger products are not affected, and users must do nothing.
