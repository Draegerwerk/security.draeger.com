### [SweynTooth Bluetooth Low Energy vulnerabilities: Dräger medical devices are not affected](<advisories/PSA-20-064-1-SweynTooth-Product-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-20-064-1 · 2020-03-05

In February 2020, researchers published 12 vulnerabilities in Bluetooth Low Energy implementations, named SweynTooth. An attacker can cause device crashes, deadlocks and security bypasses. Dräger medical devices do not use Bluetooth Low Energy, therefore they are not affected. No action is necessary.
