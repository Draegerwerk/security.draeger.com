### [Dräger Core and Dräger M540 Converter Service vulnerable to crafted, unencrypted SDC messages](<advisories/PSA-24-110-1-gSOAP-Product-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-24-110-1 · 2024-04-22

Dräger found a vulnerability in gSOAP 2.8.131. A specially crafted, unencrypted SDC message causes a high CPU load during discovery, and the service stops to process SDC messages. Core 1.0.5 and M540 Converter Service 1.0.9 are affected. A restart corrects the condition. Dräger says that no action is necessary and will correct the defect in the next release.
