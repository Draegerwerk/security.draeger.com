### [ACCESS:7 advisory: Dräger medical products are not affected](<advisories/PSA-22-76-1-Access-7-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-22-076-1 · 2022-03-17

ACCESS:7 is a group of seven vulnerabilities (CVE-2022-25246 to CVE-2022-25252) in the PTC Axeda agent and Axeda Desktop Server. Dräger medical products and their infrastructure do not use this software. Therefore Dräger devices and software are not affected and no action is necessary.
