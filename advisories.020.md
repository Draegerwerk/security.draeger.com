### [Dräger Infinity M300 VG2.x patient monitor security vulnerabilities](<advisories/2019-277-02-M300-VG2x-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-19-277-2 · 2020-07-24

Dräger found vulnerabilities in the Infinity M300 patient worn monitor with software VG2.x and earlier. A denial of service attack on the hospital network can make the monitor restart and lose its alarms and its network connection. An attacker in the Infinity network can also change alarm settings or patient data. Dräger plans software VG3.0 for 2021 and reports no patient harm.
