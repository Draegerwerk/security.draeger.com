### [Zeus IE and Zeus RS C500 Security Vulnerabilities](<advisories/Product-Security-Advisory-25-349-Zeus.pdf>)

PSA-25-349-1 · 2025-12-15

An internal penetration test showed that the USB interfaces of the Zeus IE and Zeus RS C500 anesthesia workstations can permit local attacks. A person with physical access can damage the software integrity or attack the network, with CVSS score 6.8. Dräger received no report of exploitation. Keep physical security controls and network segmentation.
