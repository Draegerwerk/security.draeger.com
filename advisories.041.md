### [CVE-2021-4034 PwnKit advisory: Infinity Central Station (ICS) VG2.1.2 and VG3](<advisories/PSA-22-63-1-ICS-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-22-063-1 · 2022-03-04

CVE-2021-4034, called PwnKit, is a memory corruption defect in the pkexec utility of polkit. A local user can get administrative rights on the machine. Infinity Central Station VG2.1.2 and VG3 are affected. Users must install the CentOS patches from February 2022: CentOS 6 for VG2 and CentOS 7 for VG3.
