### [CVE-2022-0847 Dirty Pipe advisory: Dräger medical products are not affected](<advisories/PSA-22-77-1-Dirty-Pipe-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-22-077-1 · 2022-03-18

The Dirty Pipe vulnerability (CVE-2022-0847) is in Linux kernel version 5.8 and later. A local user without privileges can get control of the system. Dräger medical products, devices and software, are not affected. No action is necessary.
