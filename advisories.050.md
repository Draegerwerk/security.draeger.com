### [Contec CMS8000 backdoor vulnerability: Dräger Vista patient monitors are not affected](<advisories/PSA-25-076-1-Product-Security-Advisory-Vista Backdoor.pdf>)

PSA-25-076-1 · 2025-03-17

The FDA and CISA reported a backdoor in the firmware of the Contec CMS8000 and Epsimed MN-120 patient monitors. Dräger examined the Vista monitor portfolio, which includes Vista 120 V3/S, Vista 120 SC, Vista 300, Vista 120 CMS and Vista CMS, with internal tests and third-party penetration tests. The Dräger Vista monitors do not have this backdoor.
