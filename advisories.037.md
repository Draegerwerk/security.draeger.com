### [Log4j advisory: Dräger medical products are not affected by CVE-2021-44228](<advisories/PSA-21-348-2-Log4J-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-21-348-2 · 2021-12-15

Apache reported a critical vulnerability in the Log4j Java logging tool (CVE-2021-44228). No Dräger medical product or its infrastructure uses the affected Log4j library. Therefore Dräger medical devices and software are not affected. Dräger will update the advisory if new findings about Log4j come.
