### [Warning: fraud attempts on Dräger customers with fake e-mail addresses](<advisories/201902-fraud-attempts-on-draeger-customers.pdf>)

PSA-19-025-1 · 2019-01-25

Customers receive fake e-mails that look like Dräger mail, from similar addresses such as dreger.com or draegers.com. The senders give a new Dräger bank account or new contact data, which can cause a money loss. Dräger sends such changes only through official channels. Customers must check with their known Dräger contact and report the fraud to i-sec@draeger.com.
