### [Infinity M300 VG2.3.1 and earlier security vulnerabilities](<advisories/2019-09-13-Cybersecurity-M300-VGxx-Security-advisory-update_v1-3.pdf>)

PSA-19-277-1 · 2019-10-09

A denial of service attack on the hospital network can make the Infinity M300 patient monitor reboot, lose its alarm function, or lose the connection to the Infinity Network. An attacker in the network could also change or switch off alarm settings. Software version VG2.3.1 and earlier are affected. Dräger planned software version VG2.3.2 for March 2020.
