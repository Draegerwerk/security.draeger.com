### [CVE-2021-22156 - BadAlloc vulnerability in the QNX operating system: Dräger medical devices are not affected](<advisories/2021-08-19-PSA-21-231-01-QNX-BadAlloc.pdf>)

PSA-21-231-1 · 2021-08-20

On 17 August 2021 BlackBerry reported that the QNX real-time operating system has the BadAlloc memory corruption vulnerability. The fault can cause a denial of service or remote code execution. No Dräger medical device or its infrastructure uses the affected operating systems in a vulnerable version. Therefore Dräger medical devices are not affected.
