### [Dräger Atlan A350 SW 1.0n security vulnerabilities](<advisories/2021-05-04-PSA-21-124-1-Security-Advisory-Atlan.pdf>)

PSA-21-124-1 · 2021-05-04

The Atlan does not check all data on the Medibus interface correctly. Non-compliant data packets can overload the internal processor after some hours. The device can then stop data transmission and show curves too late. The advisory lists Dräger Atlan A350 software 1.00 up to 1.01 as affected. Dräger received no reports of such cases from the field.
