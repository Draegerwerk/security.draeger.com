### [SolarWinds Orion hack SUNBURST - Dräger medical devices are not affected](<advisories/2020-12-15-PSA-20-350-01-SolarWinds-Orion.pdf>)

PSA-20-350-1 · 2020-12-15

Attackers put a backdoor in software of SolarWinds. Dräger medical devices do not include products of this company. Some installations use SolarWinds Dameware Mini Remote Control with Dräger products, but that application is not in the list of affected products. Dräger recommends that customers look for affected products in their networks and obey the DHS directive.
