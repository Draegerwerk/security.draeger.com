### [Treck network stack vulnerabilities Ripple20: Dräger medical devices are not affected](<advisories/PSA-20-171-2-Treck-Ripple-20-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-20-171-2 · 2020-07-06

In June 2020, the security company JSOF published several vulnerabilities in the widely used Treck network stack, named Ripple20. No Dräger medical device uses this network stack, therefore Dräger medical devices are not affected. A later test also showed no problem with ICM together with a Digi Connect ES 4 version 2.24.
