### [Open source network stack vulnerabilities AMNESIA:33 - Dräger medical devices are not affected](<advisories/2020-12-13-PSA-20-348-01-AMNESIA33.pdf>)

PSA-20-348-1 · 2020-12-13

Forescout made public 33 vulnerabilities in four open source network stacks, and CISA published an advisory about them. Dräger medical devices and the related infrastructure do not use the affected network stacks on their external communication interfaces. Therefore Dräger products are not affected.
