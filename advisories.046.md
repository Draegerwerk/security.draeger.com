### [OpenSSL vulnerabilities CVE-2022-3602 and CVE-2022-3786: Dräger medical devices are not affected](<advisories/PSA-22-312-1-OpenSSL-Product-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-22-312-1 · 2022-11-10

The OpenSSL Project published CVE-2022-3602 and CVE-2022-3786 for OpenSSL versions 3.0.0 to 3.0.6. These defects can cause a crash or possibly remote code execution. Dräger medical devices do not use these OpenSSL versions. Therefore they are not affected and no action is necessary.
