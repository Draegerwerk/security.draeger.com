### [KRACK attacks: WPA2 key reinstallation vulnerabilities in Dräger Wi-Fi devices](<advisories/2017-10-18-krack-wpa2-vulnerability-security-advisory.pdf>)

PSA-17-004-1 · 2017-10-18

The KRACK attacks use a flaw in the four-way handshake of the WPA2 standard and can break the encryption of a Wi-Fi connection. Dräger uses Wi-Fi modules from other manufacturers, which had not yet answered. Dräger therefore assumes that the Infinity M300, Infinity M540, Infinity Delta series and Vista 120 S are affected.
