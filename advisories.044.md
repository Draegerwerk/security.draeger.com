### [Dräger Clinical Assistance Package (CAP 1.0) security vulnerability CVE-2022-0778 in OpenSSL](<advisories/PSA-22-154-1-OpenSSL-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-22-154-1 · 2022-06-07

CVE-2022-0778 causes an infinite loop in the OpenSSL library. A remote attacker can cause a denial of service. The affected CAP 1.0 products are Dräger CC300 1.1.2 and 1.1.3, Dräger IACS VG7.1/VG7.1.1 and Infinity Gateway VF9.0/VF9.0.1. An attack can restart the CC300, or make SDC devices and their secondary alarm data unavailable.
