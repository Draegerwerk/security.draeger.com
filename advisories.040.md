### [Safety notice: cyber security of Evita V500, Babylog VN500 and Evita V300 ventilators](<advisories/PSA-22-040-1-Evita-V-Classic-Security-Advisory.pdf>)

PSA-22-040-1 · 2022-02-10

One operating system of these ventilators can no longer get updates, so its vulnerabilities stay open. Evita V500, Babylog VN500 and Evita V300 with SW2.60 and lower are affected. Dräger tells users to limit physical access, to connect only approved devices to the USB, LAN and DVI ports, and to watch alarms. Dräger offers tools to cover unused ports.
