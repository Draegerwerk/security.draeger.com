### [Windows Secure Boot certificate expiration in Dräger IACS, Infinity Central Station and ACL PCs](<advisories/PSA-26-174-1-Product-Security-Advisory-PSA.pdf>)

PSA-26-174-1 · 2026-06-23

Three Microsoft UEFI Secure Boot certificates from 2011 expire in 2026. Affected are Infinity Acute Care System SW VG8.0 and VG9.0, Infinity Central Station VG6 on Gen 5 hardware, and ACL Medical Grade PCs with Secure Boot switched on. The devices continue to start and work normally. But they slowly lose some Secure Boot related security updates.
