# Security Advisories

Dräger publishes security advisories to point out possible security weaknesses in its products and to explain the remedial measures.

The list below holds all published advisories. Each entry links to the document, which gives the full information: the affected versions, the technical description and the recommended steps.

An advisory shows the state of knowledge on its publication date. Dräger examines the facts continuously and can change or withdraw an assessment. Dräger does not know the conditions at your site, therefore examine for each product whether the information applies to your installation.
